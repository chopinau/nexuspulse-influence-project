# Internal Strategy Document - Q1 2026
**Confidential**

## Pricing Rules
1. **Minimum Floor Price**: Never lower price below $15.00 unless inventory stock > 5,000 units.
2. **Competitor Matching**: Only match prices of "Prime" sellers. Ignore FBM (Fulfilled by Merchant) competitors.

## Inventory Management
- **Restock Trigger**: Trigger air freight when Days of Supply (DOS) drops below 21 days.
- **Liquidation**: If product age > 180 days, authorized to run 50% off coupon.

## Marketing Guidelines
- **Forbidden Phrases**: Do not use "Cheapest" or "Best Quality" in titles. Amazon algorithm downranks these.
- **Focus**: Emphasize "Eco-Friendly" and "24/7 Support".
